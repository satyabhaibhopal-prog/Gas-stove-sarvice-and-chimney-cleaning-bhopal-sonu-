🍳 About Us

We repair and service gas stoves and kitchen chimneys at your home in Bhopal. Our technician comes to your kitchen, inspects the problem and explains what needs to be done before any work starts.


We handle gas stove repair, burner fixing, gas stove servicing, chimney repair, motor and filter work, and chimney cleaning. We work on Faber, Sunflame, Glen and other common brands. We are an independent local service, not an authorised service centre.


When a repair needs parts, we work with kitchen chimney motors, metal and standard blower fans, chimney PCB boards, baffle filters, chimney duct pipes, gas stove burners and other gas stove spare parts. Part costs are confirmed with you before any work is done.
